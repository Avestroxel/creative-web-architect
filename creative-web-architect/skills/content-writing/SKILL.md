# Content Writing

## Purpose

Generate suitable draft copy adapted to the brand tone. All content is clearly sample content — never fabricate real testimonials, achievements, or certifications.

## Copy Patterns

### Hero Headlines
- **Luxury**: "Crafted for Those Who Demand Extraordinary"
- **Tech**: "The Future of [X], Built Today"
- **Creative**: "Where Ideas Become Experiences"
- **Minimal**: "Simply Better."
- **Warm**: "Made With Care, Shared With Love"

### CTAs
- Primary: "Get Started", "Explore", "Discover More", "Begin Your Journey"
- Secondary: "Learn More", "View Details", "See How It Works"
- Avoid: "Click Here", "Submit", generic "Buy Now"

### Brand Voice Guide
| Personality | Tone | Vocabulary | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luxury | Confident, refined | Crafted, curated, exceptional | Cheap, deal, discount |
| Tech | Clear, forward | Innovative, seamless, intelligent | Old-fashioned, traditional |
| Warm | Friendly, personal | Handmade, thoughtful, together | Corporate, leverage, synergy |
| Bold | Direct, energetic | Disruptive, bold, transform | Boring, standard, normal |

## Guidelines

- Mark all sample content with `[Sample Content]` comments in code
- Adapt tone to match the creative direction
- Keep headlines short (under 8 words ideal)
- Write body copy at a 8th-grade reading level
- Include microcopy for interactive states (empty, loading, error, success)

## Validation

✅ Copy matches brand voice
✅ No fabricated testimonials or achievements
✅ Sample content is clearly labeled
✅ Headlines are concise and impactful
✅ CTAs use action verbs
