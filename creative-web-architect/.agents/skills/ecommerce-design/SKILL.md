# E-commerce Design

## Purpose

Design effective e-commerce experiences with beautiful product presentation, intuitive navigation, and conversion-optimized workflows.

## Key Pages

1. **Homepage** — Hero + featured products + categories + trust signals
2. **Product Listing** — Grid/list view, filters, sort, pagination
3. **Product Detail** — Gallery, description, variants, add-to-cart, reviews
4. **Cart** — Line items, quantities, totals, continue shopping
5. **Checkout** — Minimal steps, progress indicator, guest option

## Product Grid Patterns

- 2 columns mobile, 3 tablet, 4 desktop
- Quick-add overlay on hover
- Wishlist button
- Price and rating visible

## Validation

✅ Products are easy to browse and filter
✅ Cart is always accessible
✅ Checkout has minimal friction
✅ Mobile shopping experience is excellent
✅ Trust signals are visible (security, returns, shipping)
